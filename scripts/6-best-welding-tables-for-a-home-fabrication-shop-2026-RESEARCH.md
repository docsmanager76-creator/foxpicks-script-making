## 2. Buyer-decision checklist
1. Top thickness (listing gauge or mm): thin tops warp under sustained heat and grinding.
2. Flatness of the top out of the box (listing never states it; check with a straightedge).
3. Hole size and spacing: 5/8 in (15.9 mm) vs 16 mm; third-party pins and clamps must match.
4. Capacity is a maker claim; static, not hammering.
5. Size and height: 36x24 fixed, 42x21 folding, 36x18 narrow; working height and adjustability.
6. Fixed vs folding vs casters: stability against storage.
7. Frame/leg thickness and feet or levelling.
8. What is in the box (fit-up kit, clamps, tool slots, shelf) and assembly effort.
9. Warranty term.
10. Heat: welding vs plasma/oxy cutting (slag damage), cold-rolled vs galvanized top (zinc fumes when welded; UNVERIFIED, general welding knowledge, not claimed in script).

## 9. Free upgrade (sourced)
(1) Lay a straightedge across the top before the first weld; thin tops are often not flat (forum). (2) Measure a hole and your pin; 16 mm is not exactly 5/8 in (seller guidance). (3) Level the feet and recheck. (4) Tack, do not clamp rigid, so heat can move. (5) Keep grinding off the middle of the top. One minute.
