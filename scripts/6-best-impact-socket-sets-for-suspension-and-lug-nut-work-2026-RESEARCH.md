## 3. Buyer checklist
1. Drive size: 1/2 in for lug and axle nuts; 3/8 in is too small.
2. Impact-rated (Cr-Mo or heat-treated), black finish; not chrome.
3. 6-point, not 12-point.
4. Sizes: lug nuts 17/19/21/22 mm and 3/4, 13/16, 7/8, 15/16 in; suspension 10-24 mm; axle nuts 29-38 mm.
5. Deep vs shallow reach for studs and long bolts.
6. Wall thickness and wheel protection (thin-wall or sleeve for alloy wheels).
7. Extension and case; warranty.

## 5. Hook — type 2 Data Wall
Lug nuts take 17 to 22 mm; axle nuts take 29 to 38 mm; and sets in this video run from 5 pieces to 65. No single set in the video covers both jobs, so you pick by job. Open loops: villain, free upgrade, which set covers which job.

## 6. Myth
"A big impact set covers wheel and suspension work." The widest set here stops at 24 mm (Neiko) or 30 mm (Sunex 2569); axle nuts start at 29 mm; the villain's metric range stops at 19 mm.

## 7. Villain — type C, outlet-named: Sunex 3580

## 9. Free upgrade
Check the wheel nut size in the manual, measure axle nut and the socket depth, use a 6-point socket, push the socket fully on before the impact, short bursts, final torque with a wrench to spec, check torque after 50 miles, keep the case in order. No purchase.
