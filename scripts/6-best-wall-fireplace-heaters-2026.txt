##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and you do it before you mount anything.

Read the clearance number in your fireplace's manual. These makers do not agree. Touchstone says eight inches above the Sideline. Westinghouse asks for almost twelve above its model. The Bernice hides its vents so a TV can go right above it. Now measure from the top of the fireplace to the bottom edge of the TV, not to the wall bracket. A bracket can hide inches of screen. Check where the heat blows, out the top front or out of sight. And plug straight into a wall outlet, never an extension cord or a power strip. Fire safety groups say the same for every heater.

That measurement costs nothing, and it decides your whole wall.
