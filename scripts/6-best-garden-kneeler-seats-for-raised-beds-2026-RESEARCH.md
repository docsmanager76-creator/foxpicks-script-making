## FREE UPGRADE — the tape-measure check
1. Measure your bed's top edge. Per NC State, 8–12 in beds are kneeling height and 2–3 ft beds are seated height.
2. Seated reach is about 30 in (Chicago Botanic Garden), so beds worked from one side stay about 2 ft wide, or about 4 ft if you can reach from both sides (Seattle P-Patch / NCHPAD guidance).
3. On mulch paths, expect sinking: Which? and Bob Vila both saw it with a framed seat. Set it on the path or a flat paver, not the loose mulch. The paver tip is my inference and will be phrased as a tip, not a test.
4. Hose it off and dry it before folding (Bob Vila).
