##HOOK

It beat two benches rated for a thousand. That is not a typo, and it is the whole lesson. On a clamping bench, the clamp is the product. The weight rating mostly tells you what it carries while nobody is working on it. You will hear why a capacity number is the wrong way to shop for one of these. ##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes thirty seconds.

The enemy is the edge of the bench. Klein rates its thousand pounds as evenly distributed, and that fine print matters on every bench here. Weight hanging past the edge acts like a lever over the far legs, and a light folding bench tips long before it breaks. So set the heavy part over the legs, then close the clamps. Now do what Bob Vila's testers did. Pry on the clamped board, push each corner, and listen for every latch to click. One bench in that test had a clamp that never locked. You want to find that before your saw does.

That habit costs nothing, and it decides whether the work stays put or ends up on the floor.

##TAIL
Before you pay, ask one question: what will you clamp most, a board on its edge or a flat panel? Edge work wants vise jaws. Panels want a wide top and long bar clamps. No spec sheet asks.
