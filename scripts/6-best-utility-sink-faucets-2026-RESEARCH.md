## L1 HOOK — Type **6 Category Lie** (last script used 3; 6 is allowed)
Every utility faucet listing sells the threaded spout: "connect your hose." Here's the raw material:
- A popular listing's own title says **"3/4 in. NPT threaded end."** A garden hose is **3/4 in. GHT**: 11.5 threads per inch, straight, vs NPT's 14 per inch, tapered. Gates states GHT is not compatible with 3/4-14 NPT.
- Moen's own spec sheet for Bob Vila's Best Overall says: "Some local plumbing codes may require installation of a backflow preventer."
- Code readings (Jaspector on the IRC; Sacramento County EMD; Wisconsin DNR): a threaded laundry/utility spout needs a hose-connection vacuum breaker.
- Hook names the outlet (Bob Vila) and Moen as the spec-sheet author, but **no pick names**.

## FREE UPGRADE (~127-130 words)
Before any hose touches a threaded spout:
1. Check the thread. A GHT hose fits GHT, so use an adapter if the label says NPT.
2. Screw on a hose-connection vacuum breaker (ASSE 1011). It's the brass cap sold for outdoor spigots.
3. Never leave the hose end sitting in a bucket of bleach or detergent.

Sources: Gates thread chart, ICC PMG CodeNotes (backflow), Jaspector IRC 2021/2024 reading, Sacramento County EMD, Wisconsin DNR. Present it as "check your local code."
