##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and you do it before you buy anything.

Find the amps on the heater's label or listing. Multiply by one and a quarter. The electrical code treats a fixed heater as a load that runs for hours, so its wiring is sized at one hundred twenty five percent. Take number one. Twelve and a half amps times one and a quarter is about fifteen and a half, so it needs a twenty amp circuit, not fifteen. Then read the breaker that would feed it. Electricians argue whether that rule covers the breaker or only the wire, so your electrician decides. For a plug in heater, skip the extension cord and the power strip.

That check costs nothing, and it can save you a melted wire.
