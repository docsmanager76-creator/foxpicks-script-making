##FREEUPGRADE
Now the free thing, and it works on any bench that adjusts.

Set the height to your body. Stand with your arms relaxed, measure the floor to your wrist, and take off an inch or two.

Then set it to the job. Fine Woodworking has been saying this for years. Standard benches sit near thirty six inches. Planing wants lower, because you push with your upper body. Sawing dovetails and carving are easier on your back higher up.

That is what twenty eight and a half inches at number five was for. A planing height.

Bob Vila puts the useful band at thirty two to forty inches, with three to four feet of clearance around it.

Four of these six adjust in seconds. Almost nobody moves them off the factory setting.
