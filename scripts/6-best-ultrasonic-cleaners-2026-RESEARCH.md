## 6. Myth — "higher kHz or 'professional grade' means cleaner"
- Listings: 46, 48, 50 kHz "professional". The two outlets' winners: Magnasonic 42 (RD, BestReviews), Ukoke 40/42, VisiClean 42, VEVOR 40 (RD).
- Trade guides (Sparklean, iultrasonic): 40-42 for jewelry; higher is gentler and slower. Maker/vendor sources, so attributed ("one jewelry seller's guide", "a maker's guide").
- What RD found actually mattered: heat on greasy grime (VEVOR watch at 100°F) and cycle length.
- **No lab measured cleaning vs kHz.** The script must say frequency is a spec, not a result.

## 9. Free upgrade
- Four free rules: (a) run a degas first on every fresh fill (Stuller; LifeBasis does 20 s automatically) (b) add a drop of dish soap (RD used it on the Ukoke caps, the LifeBasis sunglasses, and AOL used it on the brush) (c) keep pearls, opals and emeralds out (Sparklean: nacre cracks, opals are porous, emerald fillers strip) (d) on coated lenses keep cycles short: a seller guide reports coating separation past about 3 minutes, UNVERIFIED by any optical body. Ask the lens maker.
- Do not claim a lifespan or damage rate.
