## 3. Buyer checklist (laminate and vinyl plank cutters)
1. Max thickness vs your plank (12, 13, 15.8, 16, 17 mm).
2. Max width (all 13 in).
3. Material: laminate and LVP vs engineered wood (Janka limit).
4. Angle cuts: stops (15/22.5/30/45/90) vs movable fence.
5. Weight and carry (13.8 to 20.5 lb listed).
6. Blade: replaceable, spare included, sharpening stone.
7. Safety lock for the blade.
8. Warranty term; support.

## 5. Hook — type 6, Category Lie
"Laminate flooring cutter" is the search term, but listings sell mostly for vinyl plank, and every one says 13 inches wide. What decides fit is thickness, 12 to 17 mm, and weight, 13.8 to 20.5 lb. (Rotation: not hook 5.) Hook ≤150 words, open loops: villain, free upgrade.

## 6. Myth
"A cutter that says laminate will cut every laminate and wood floor." Limits are thickness, width and hardness: Marshalltown states engineered wood only to 3/8 in and Janka 1,200.

## 9. Free upgrade (≈128–135 words)
Measure plank thickness with calipers and check width and species hardness against the listing; cut a scrap first; support the whole plank on the table; make one steady pull; keep the blade sharp with the included stone; lock the blade when idle; wear gloves. No purchase needed; ties to the hook's thickness spread.
