##FREEUP
Now the part nobody mentions, and it costs nothing.

Before you buy any of these, take a kitchen knife, cut a wedge of turf and lift it. You want the spongy brown layer between the green grass and the soil. Measure it.

Under half an inch, put the knife down and keep your money. At that depth thatch is helping you: it limits weed germination, slows evaporation and protects roots from frost. Over half an inch, it is a problem, and a machine earns its place.

That is Minnesota, Oklahoma State, Clemson and Missouri extension, all landing on the same half inch.

When you do it matters as much as whether. Cool season grass wants late summer or early fall, which is right now. Warm season wants spring, after green-up. Dethatch at the wrong end of the year and you tear a lawn open with no growing season left to close it.

Two minutes, no cost, and the only advice here that might save you the whole purchase.
