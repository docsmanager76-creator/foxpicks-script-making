##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it works with any stand on this list.

These tips come from maker guidance and common practice, not a lab test.

First, measure your roll against the stand. The listings give five and a half inches for one and six inches for another.

Second, if your stand has a reserve, load the spare rolls low and the working roll on top, because a top-heavy stand tips more.

Third, check the pad or feet on your floor. If it rocks, move it.

Fourth, tighten the screws, and on the KES, unscrew the pre-installed screw and washer first.

Fifth, put it against a wall, away from where people walk.

Sixth, wipe it with a damp cloth, and use the spring dowel on the Gatco to change rolls.
