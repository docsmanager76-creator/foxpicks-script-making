Read past the headline on almost any listing in this category, and the manufacturer's own fine print quietly walks that promise back. Short grass only. Dry debris only. Flat ground only. Not this, not that, not the other thing.

That's not one honest company being upfront. That's the pattern across the entire category, and almost nobody puts it in the headline where you'd actually see it before you click buy. So I sorted five real sweepers by what they're actually built to do, not what the title promises. I'll show you exactly where each one's fine print kicks in. ##FREE
One habit costs nothing, and every maker in this category quietly agrees on it.

Set the brushes so they just barely touch the tips of the grass, not dig into it. Every listing in this video, in its own words, warns that brushes set too low bind in thick grass and get harder to push. That's exactly the failure Agri-Fab owners keep reporting, worn adjustment hardware and all.

The fix costs nothing beyond checking it before you start. Do it at the beginning of every session, not just once at assembly, because grass height changes week to week even on the same lawn.
