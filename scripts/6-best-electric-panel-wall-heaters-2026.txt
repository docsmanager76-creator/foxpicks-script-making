##HOOK
Almost every wall heater box in this category sells you savings. One promises up to fifty percent off your heating bill. Another claims thirty seven percent. Now read what the Department of Energy says about electric resistance heat. All of the electricity that goes in comes out as heat. One hundred percent, for every heater on this list. So a five hundred watt panel cannot make more heat than five hundred watts, however slim it looks. The savings are real, but they come from somewhere else, and you will hear where. You will also hear the spec on the box that misleads buyers most. A famous little heater Bob Vila liked is my villain, and a free circuit check matters more than any heater. ##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it is about your breaker, not your heater.

A wall heater runs for hours, and electricians treat that as a continuous load. The National Electrical Code limits a continuous load to eighty percent of the circuit. On a common fifteen amp circuit, that is twelve amps. A fifteen hundred watt heater already pulls twelve and a half on its own. So before you mount anything, flip the breaker and see what else goes dark. If that circuit also runs a television or a computer, pick a lower wattage panel, or use another circuit. Two five hundred watt panels on separate circuits can beat one big heater fighting a busy one.

That check costs nothing, and it decides whether your heater runs all winter or trips the breaker every night.
