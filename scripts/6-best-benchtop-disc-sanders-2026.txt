##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it stops the most common wrong order.

First, measure your bench and your lift. These machines run from about forty pounds to nearly eighty-four, and the heavy ones need two people.

Second, hold your largest workpiece against the disc size. A wide panel needs a twelve inch disc, as at numbers six and one.

Third, hook the dust port to a shop vacuum before the first cut. The Hoteche manual says to use dust collection, and it applies to all of them.

Fourth, check the disc. Spin it by hand and hold a straightedge near the face. If it wobbles or is out of true, return it before you sand.

That costs nothing, and it takes ten minutes. Do it before you order.
