## 9. Free upgrade — fall battery care
- Before the first freeze: store the pack at roughly 40-60% charge indoors, above freezing, not in an unheated shed (Consumer Reports, STIHL guide). EGO dealer blog says 30-50%; makers differ, so check the manual.
- Also: pull wet leaf and stem wrap off the head and shaft before storing; reload fresh line so spring starts clean.
