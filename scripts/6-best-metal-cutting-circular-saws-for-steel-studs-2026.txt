##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes one minute.

First, check the arbor size and the blade size before you buy spare blades.

Second, if the saw is tool only, plan for a battery and a charger.

Third, clamp the stud, and support both sides of the cut.

Sixth, thin track is a special case. Pro Tool Reviews found that thin drywall studs and twenty to twenty five gauge track cut better on an abrasive saw than on a dry cut chop saw. That was a sponsored chop saw test, so treat it as a caution, and make a test cut first.
