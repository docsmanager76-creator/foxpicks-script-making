## STEP 6 — THREE PROMISES (proposed)
- **Hook: 1 COLLISION.** Several picks burn exactly 30,000 BTU yet makers claim 1,000 vs 1,400 sq ft, and Mr. Heater's own listing says 1,000 while retailers print 750. Name outlet: Bob Vila (Nov 2026), Energy.gov. Ledger: last = hook 4 / villain D, so 1 is allowed.
- **Myth:** "BTU / 99.9% efficient = better." Vent-free burns all heat into the room, but adds moisture and NOx (Energy.gov, LBNL) while direct vent sits ~81% AFUE (retailer; 80/81/82 conflict) yet never touches room air. NO measured head-to-head found: myth can only be proved with government/lab air-quality findings, not a spec-sheet-leader-loses test. Flag: weaker than normal.
- **Villain: UNVERIFIED. STOP.** No outlet-recommends-but-testing-contradicts product found. Options below.
