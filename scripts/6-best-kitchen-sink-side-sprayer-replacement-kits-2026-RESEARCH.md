## Buyer-decision checklist
1. Hose connection on YOUR faucet: threaded (male or female), quick connect (2007 on), direct connect (limited 2018 models). 2. Does the kit include head + hose + holder, or head only, or hose only. 3. Thread size (1/4-18 NPSM female, 1/2-14 NPSM at the sprayer end) and adapters included (Moen quick connect, 1/4" and 3/8" barbs for Delta). 4. Hose length (about 47 in vs 60 in) and hose material (PVC vs braided). 5. Head: thumb on/off, spray modes, material (ABS/plastic vs metal). 6. Finish match (chrome, nickel, bronze, black). 7. Warranty. 8. Install steps: shut off water, hand-tighten, do not split a preassembled unit.

## Free upgrade
1) Shut off water, look under the sink, identify the hose style first (threaded male/female, quick connect with white or black tab, direct connect). 2) Photograph the connector and the faucet model before ordering. 3) Replace head and hose as one unit when the kit is preassembled. 4) Hand-tighten, use thread tape where the listing says to, check for kinks. 5) Weak spray: check the head for mineral buildup. Verify wording against Moen's article; generic troubleshooting pages are weak sources.
