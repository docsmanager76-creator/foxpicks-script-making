## Buyer-decision checklist (Kohler toilets)
1. Flush performance: MaP grams. 1000 g is the test ceiling, not a rating.
2. Water use: gpf, WaterSense, single vs dual flush.
3. Bowl height: Comfort Height (chair height) or standard.
4. Bowl shape: elongated, compact elongated, or round.
5. Rough-in: 12 in standard. 10 and 14 in exist.
6. One-piece vs two-piece: cleaning and weight.
7. Trapway: exposed, skirted or concealed, which drives cleaning time; also trapway diameter.
8. Flush tech: AquaPiston/Class Five canister (no flapper), pressure-assist, or powered.
9. Seat included? Quiet-Close?
10. Smart: bidet, power needs, emergency flush, minimum supply flow.
11. Warranty.
12. Extras: tank locks, Insuliner liner, no-drill skirted install, water-surface size.

## Hook — type 5, Single Absurd Number (last row was 4 → OK)
**"1,000."** Of 2,792 scored tank toilets in the MaP lab list, 2,091 (75%) hit 1,000 grams. Of 116 scored Kohlers, 96 hit it. 1,000 g is where the test **stops**, not a grade. So the number on the box can't pick your toilet. The trapway, the height, what's in the box and the flush type can.

## Myth
"Highest MaP score = best toilet." MaP tests to failure and caps at 1000 g. Its own FAQ says ≥500 g is exceptional, and WaterSense passes at 350 g. Kohler's own list still has spread: 19 models at 800 g and one at 600 g (Highline K-26812). Payoff: #3 Leap scores 800 and is still a right pick.
