##FREE
Here is the free upgrade.

Cover the top, not the sides. Firewood guides agree that a cover belongs over the top of the stack, while open sides let air dry the wood. Sealing it up can trap moisture. Number one's cover is built that way.

Stack the longest logs on the bottom row, as those testers advise, and keep the rack on level, well drained ground. Keep the bottom row off the soil to limit moisture and pests. Leave small gaps between pieces so air can move.

Then keep the stack away from your siding. Family Handyman says twenty feet or more, to discourage pests. Other guides range from three feet to thirty, so check your local fire code.

Do that, and any rack here keeps your wood drier.
