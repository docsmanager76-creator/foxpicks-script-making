##FREEUPGRADE
Now the free check, and it takes about thirty seconds before you ever compare two lasers side by side.

Every accuracy claim in this video comes as a fraction of an inch next to a distance — not just the fraction alone. That distance is the part people skip reading.

Here's the habit. Before you decide one laser is more precise than another, find the distance. Each accuracy figure was measured at some specific distance. If they don't match, convert both to the same distance, or just divide the fraction by the distance to get a per-foot number you can actually compare.

A tighter fraction at a shorter distance isn't automatically better than a looser fraction measured further out. Sometimes it's the exact same laser, described two different ways.
