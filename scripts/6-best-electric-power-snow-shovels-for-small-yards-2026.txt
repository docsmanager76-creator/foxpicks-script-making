##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes one minute. It comes from the makers' own notes.

First, keep your battery indoors at room temperature. Greenworks warns that cold batteries can struggle to start.

Second, charge it fully the night before the storm, not the morning of.

Third, go out early. These shovels are rated for about five to eight inches, so clear twice in a big storm.

Fourth, with a corded shovel, use an outdoor rated cord, and keep it behind you, away from the auger.

Fifth, if it clogs, pull the battery or the plug first. Never clear it with your hand. Use a stick.

Sixth, wipe the snow off before you store it, so the switch and motor stay dry.

That costs nothing, and it takes one minute.
