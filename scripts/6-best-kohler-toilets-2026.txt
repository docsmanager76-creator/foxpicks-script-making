##FREEUPGRADE
Now the free check, and it takes one minute before you shop.

Measure your rough-in. That's the distance from the wall to the center of the bolts that hold your old toilet down. Toilets are built around that number, because the drain under the bowl has to land on the pipe in your floor. Measure from the finished wall, not the baseboard. Twelve inches is standard, and all six toilets here are twelve-inch models. If you get ten or fourteen, you need a different model, or Kohler's trap kit on the Tresham.

While you're down there, check for an outlet if you want the smart toilet, and look at the water valve.

That check costs nothing, and it decides whether you install a toilet or return one.
