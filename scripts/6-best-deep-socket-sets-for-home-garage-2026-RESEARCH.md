## 2. Buyer-decision checklist
1. Drive size: 1/4 (dash, small), 3/8 (most jobs), 1/2 (axle, lug, high torque).
2. Deep vs shallow and length (deep adds reach, not strength; extra-deep 4.5 in to 4.75 in).
3. 6-point vs 12-point (6-point grips flats, rounds less).
4. Impact-rated (Cr-Mo / black or phosphate finish) vs chrome (polished, hand tools only).
5. SAE, metric or both, and size range with no gaps.
6. Wall thickness/fit on fasteners (Bob Vila; listings do not give wall thickness).
7. Case or rail, markings, number of pieces.
8. Warranty term.
9. Do not mix: chrome on impact gun.
10. Match to the job: spark plugs, valve cover, brake caliper.
