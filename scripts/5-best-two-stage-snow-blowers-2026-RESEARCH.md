## 3. Buyer checklist (two-stage snow blowers)
1. Clearing width and intake height.
2. Power: gas displacement (cc) or battery platform and count.
3. Machine weight (and with/without batteries).
4. Throw distance and chute control.
5. Self-propel speeds and reverse.
6. Start system (recoil, electric start, push button).
7. Runtime (battery) or fuel tank / stated capacity.
8. Tires / traction.
9. Cold-weather comforts: lights, hand warmers.
10. Warranty and what is excluded (batteries, engine).
11. Maintenance: shear pins, fuel stabilizer.
12. What is not in the box (EGO: battery and charger).
