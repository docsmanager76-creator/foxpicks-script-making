##FREE
One habit costs nothing and it's the one thing every independent test in this video actually agrees on.

Full power isn't the right setting for anything you're melting or gently warming rather than reheating. One consumer testing group's own side-by-side found every inverter microwave could melt chocolate cleanly. The standard microwave in that same test scorched it into something they called unusable. The mechanism is simple: a standard microwave cycles full power on and off even at a low setting. Delicate food gets hit with a full burst regardless of the number on the dial, over and over, for the length of the cook.

The habit: for butter, chocolate, custard, or anything you're softening, use the lowest power level or a dedicated melt and soften setting instead of full power for less time. It takes longer. But it's the one scenario every test here agrees inverter tech is actually built for.
