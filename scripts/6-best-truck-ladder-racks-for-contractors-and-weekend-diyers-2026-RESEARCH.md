## 6. Myth: "universal fit means it fits your truck"
- Every budget listing says "universal" and then says it does not fit a tonneau cover, toolbox or utility track. Modern trucks often carry one of the three.
- etrailer lists most fixed racks (Thule TracONE, Buyers, Thule T-Rac Pro2, Yakima 500 lb) as **not tonneau compatible**; only the Thule SR sliding and the Yakima 300 lb version with adapters are. The SR is the flagship tier and is not a pick.
- "800 lb" is also not one number: Thule says properly installed and evenly distributed; MaxxHaul "across both uprights"; Yakima 500 on-road, 300 off-road.
