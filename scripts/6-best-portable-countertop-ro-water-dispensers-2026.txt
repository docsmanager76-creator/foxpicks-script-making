##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it works on every machine on this list.

Change the tap water in the feed tank every day. Do not top it up. Dump it and refill it.

That tank holds still water at room temperature, sometimes for days. Philips and SimPure both say it in their own listings: replace the source tank water daily. One reviewer skipped the daily rinse on a Waterdrop A2, and a film formed in the tank. DREO even warns you when water has sat for two days.

Once a month, run the built-in flush or self-clean to push out scale.

Going away for a week? Empty the tank and drain the machine.

That habit costs nothing, and it decides whether you drink clean water or filtered stale water.
