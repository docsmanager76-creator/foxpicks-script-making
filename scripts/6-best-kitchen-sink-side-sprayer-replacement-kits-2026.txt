##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it stops the most common wrong order.

First, shut off the water and look under the sink. Moen's guide says to identify the hose style first. Threaded, quick connect, and direct connect parts do not swap.

Second, take a photo of the connector and the faucet model. If the connector has a tab, note the color. A black connector has a white tab, and a white connector has a black tab.

Third, if the kit is preassembled, install the head and hose as one unit. Moen warns that separating them may cause problems.

Fourth, hand-tighten, then check the hose for kinks.

Fifth, if spray is weak, check the head for buildup.

That costs nothing, and it takes ten minutes. Do it before you order.
