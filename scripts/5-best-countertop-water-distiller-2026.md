## THE FREE UPGRADE

Now the maintenance job I promised, and it is free.

Every machine here fights the same enemy, and it is not bacteria. It is scale. Distilling concentrates every mineral from your tap water into the bottom of the tank, and it bakes onto the heating element a little more each cycle. It is why these ship with cleaner in the box, and why that thirty-nine hour test included a scale removal round.

So descale on a schedule, not when you can see it. Once the crust is visible it has been insulating the element for weeks, and an insulated element runs hotter and slower to do the same job.

That habit costs nothing, and it decides whether you replace a distiller or keep one.
