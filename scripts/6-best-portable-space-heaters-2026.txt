##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it is about your wall, not your heater.

The National Fire Protection Association says space heaters and heating stoves are behind seventy three percent of home heating fire deaths. So plug the heater straight into a wall outlet, never into an extension cord or a power strip. A fifteen hundred watt heater pulls a heavy load for hours. Check that the plug fits tight in the outlet, and if it is loose, use another one. Then keep anything that can burn three feet from the front, sides, and back. Consumer Reports adds two more rules. Set it on a hard, level floor, and do not rely on a timer while you sleep.

That check costs nothing, and it decides whether your heater stays safe.
