##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it stops the most common wrong order.

First, open your RO system manual and find out whether it is air gap or non-air gap. A non-air gap faucet cannot connect to an air gap system, and the reverse is also true.

Second, measure your sink hole and your countertop thickness. Compare both to the faucet, as you saw at numbers six, three, and two.

Third, check your local plumbing code. California's requires an air gap, but the section number differs by edition, so confirm yours.

Fourth, if the faucet leaks at the base, check the drain tube first. One owner traced a leak to a clogged drain tube, not the faucet.

That costs nothing, and it takes ten minutes. Do it before you order.
