##FREEUPGRADE
Now the free test, and it works on every sprayer in this video.

NC State Extension calls it the 128th of an acre method.

Mark out three hundred and forty square feet. That is one 128th of an acre. Divide three hundred and forty by the width of your spray band, and that is how far you walk.

Fill the tank with plain water. Walk that distance at your normal pace, spraying, and time it.

Then stand still and spray into a measuring cup for the same number of seconds.

The ounces in the cup are your gallons per acre. Exactly. An ounce is one 128th of a gallon, so the maths does itself.

Now every rate on every product label means something, whichever sprayer you own.
