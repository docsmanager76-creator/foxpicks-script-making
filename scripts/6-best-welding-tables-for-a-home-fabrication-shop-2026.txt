##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes one minute.

First, lay a straightedge across the top before the first weld. One forum poster says thin tops are often not flat to begin with.

Second, measure one hole and one pin. Remember sixteen millimeters versus five eighths? One table seller says measure before you buy tooling.

Third, level the feet, then check the straightedge again. A rocking table pulls your parts out of square.

Fourth, grind any coating off where your ground clamp sits. Bare steel gives a clean, steady arc.

Fifth, keep heavy heat off the middle of a thin top. One forum poster heated a three sixteenths inch top with a torch, and it was no longer flat.

That costs nothing, and it takes one minute.
