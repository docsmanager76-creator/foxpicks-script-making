##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes one minute. It comes from the New York State Department of Health.

First, burn only K one kerosene. K two can look just as clear, but it carries more sulfur. Never use gasoline.

Second, never refuel a hot heater. Let it cool first.

Third, check the wick every week. Clean it if it is dirty, and replace it if it is worn.

Fourth, do not burn last season's kerosene. Old fuel can change and burn badly.

Fifth, install a carbon monoxide alarm and a smoke alarm, and do not buy a heater too big for the room.

Sixth, do not make it your main heat, even in an outage, and give it fresh air while it runs.

That costs nothing, and it takes one minute.
