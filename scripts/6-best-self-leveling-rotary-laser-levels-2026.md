##FREE
Run this check before you trust any of these six on a real job. It's called the two-peg test — the industry's own standard field check for a rotary or survey-grade laser, not a marketing claim. Set the laser midway between two points about a hundred feet apart, and mark the beam height at both. Move the laser next to one of those points and mark again. Compare the two height differences. Manufacturer manuals, DeWalt's included, flag more than a quarter inch of drift over that distance as a send-it-for-calibration signal, not a reason to return it.

A rotary laser that drifts without telling you isn't self-leveling. It's guessing with extra confidence. That habit costs nothing, and it decides whether you catch a bad pour before it's poured, or after.
