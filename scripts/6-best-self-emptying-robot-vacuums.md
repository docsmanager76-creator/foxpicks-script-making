##FREEUPGRADE
Now the free fix, and it works on every self-emptying robot here.

Roborock's own support page lists the reasons a dock does not empty. One of them catches almost everyone.

The robot will not auto-empty after returning to the dock in Do Not Disturb mode.

Here is why that bites. Docks are loud. TechGearLab measured them between sixty two and eighty decibels while emptying. So people turn on Do Not Disturb for the night, and then schedule the clean inside that window. The robot comes home full, and the dock stays silent.

Fix it in the app. Let the clean finish before Do Not Disturb starts, or press the dock button to empty by hand.

And since no robot passed all five of TechGearLab's obstacle tests, spend sixty seconds picking up cords and socks first.
