##FREE
One habit costs nothing. It prevents the single most common way any drill on this list actually fails a fastener.

It's called cam-out: the bit slips and rounds off the screw head, or its own tip, because the clutch let through more torque than that fastener could take. Pro Tool Reviews' own explainer on drill clutches describes exactly this failure. It happens on soft pine and hardwood alike, just for different reasons at each end.

Before the real fastener goes into the real material, drive one test screw into a scrap offcut of that same material, starting from a low clutch number. Move up one setting at a time until the screw sets flush, without the clutch slipping early or the head stripping.

That calibration takes less time than digging a stripped screw out with pliers, and it works no matter what a drill's torque number claims. Costs nothing, and it's the one number in this whole video you set yourself instead of trusting the listing.
