##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes one minute. It comes from the makers' own setup notes.

First, check the engine oil before every use. Honda puts this at the top of its list.

Second, check the fuel. Use fresh gas, and add a stabilizer if it will sit.

Third, look inside the auger housing and the chute, and make sure they are clear.

Fourth, set the skid shoes for your surface. Raise them on gravel, so the machine does not throw stones.

Fifth, if the chute clogs, shut the engine off first. Never clear it with your hand. Use a clearing tool.

Sixth, plug in electric start machines before the first cold morning, not during the storm.

That costs nothing, and it takes one minute.
