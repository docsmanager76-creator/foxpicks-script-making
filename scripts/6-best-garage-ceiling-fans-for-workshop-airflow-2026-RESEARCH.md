## 6. Myth — "an industrial-style ceiling fan is a garage ceiling fan"
- The Westinghouse "Industrial-Style" Jax page says "Indoor 56 inch", Room Type Bedroom, Dining Room, Living Room, and no damp rating, though Bob Vila named it best ceiling for garages.
- Ohniyou's title says "Industrial ... for Patio, Porch" while a bullet says shop or garage; Fanbulous says Style Industrial, Recommended Uses: Living Room.
- Only Hunter Cassius states "damp-rated for covered outdoor areas like patios or garages".
- Proof stays modest: no outlet tested damp ratings, and Bob Vila's own caveat is wiring. Sources on damp ratings are retailer buying guides (Lowe's: damp rated for humid spaces, wet rated for direct water, dry for indoor only).

## 7. Villain — type C, famous name: Minka-Aire Barn 65 in
- **Recommended by:** Bob Vila (Best Overall, 2025-11-17: quiet, easy install, reversible blades, smart control, strong airflow, 3,712-9,155 CFM).
- **Failure (sourced):** Bob Vila lists it as **dry rated, indoor only**, with an awkward dual-button remote for dimming; its headline CFM is the top of a 2.5x range. For a garage the problem is the rating, not performance.
- **Weakness:** no outlet tested it in a garage and no failure was measured: it is "right fan, wrong room". Said openly. It is a wrong-job villain like the NOCO GB40.
- Link: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07ND365CZ
- **Backup villain (A/B):** Hunter Hartland 52 (Bob Vila Best for Large Rooms: bulbs too dim, blades not reversible, nearly an hour to install) — weaker.
