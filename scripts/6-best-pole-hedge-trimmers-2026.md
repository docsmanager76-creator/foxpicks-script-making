##FREE
One habit costs nothing and matters more than every spec in this video combined.

A cordless pole hedge trimmer's own instruction manual states it plainly. To protect yourself from electrocution, do not operate within ten feet of overhead electrical lines. Another manufacturer's manual goes further: never use an extended-reach hedge trimmer near power lines at all. Before you extend the pole, look up and trace the actual line it will sweep through, not just where the hedge ends. Ten feet, minimum, from anything overhead, every single time.

That's the one number in this whole video where guessing wrong doesn't cost you a returned box. It costs you a hospital trip.
