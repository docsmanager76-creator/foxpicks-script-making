## Buyer-decision checklist
1. Strap type: static polyester (about 10% stretch or less, for flat pulls/towing) vs nylon snatch strap (about 20-25% stretch) vs kinetic rope (30-35% claimed).
2. Break strength vs working load limit (WLL). Only ALL-TOP's listing states a WLL (12,000 lb).
3. Size to vehicle weight: 2-3x (Unsealed 4X4, Rhino) or 3.5-4x (The Drive); Overland Journal podcast says kinetic rope 1.5-2x GVW. **These conflict.**
4. Length, width, eye reinforcement and protector sleeves.
5. Shackles included and their rating (soft vs steel).
6. Rated recovery points; never a tow ball (Overland Journal).
7. Warranty; storage bag.
