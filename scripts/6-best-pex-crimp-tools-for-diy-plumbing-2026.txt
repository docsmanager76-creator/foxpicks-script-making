##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes one minute before you close the wall.

First, match the ring to the tool. Remember the hook? A tool for one cannot make the other joint.

Second, check every joint with a go/no-go gauge. The testers say it should slip about halfway on, then stop. If it slides all the way over, or will not go on, the joint may leak.

Third, on cinch clamps, confirm the ear is fully closed. One outlet says that is how SharkBite checks it.

Fourth, if your tool has an adjustment, check the calibration before the job. Overtightened jaws can cause leaks.

Fifth, keep the expander on the shelf unless your pipe is Type A.

That costs nothing, and it takes one minute.
