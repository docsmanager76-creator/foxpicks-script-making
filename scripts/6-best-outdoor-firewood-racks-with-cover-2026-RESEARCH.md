## Buyer-decision checklist
1. Rack size and cord capacity (1/4, 1/2, 3/4 cord), matched to how much you burn.
2. Frame steel gauge/thickness and finish (powder coat at minimum, per Family Handyman).
3. Cover coverage: top-only (airflow) vs wrap-around; cover fabric (600D Oxford, PU/PVC layer), vents, straps.
4. Ground clearance and stability (feet sink in soft ground; level area needed for long racks).
5. Rated load vs weight of a stack (listing load numbers collide).
6. Warranty and where it is made.
7. Placement: raised off the ground, level, spaced from the house (sources disagree on distance).

- **Hook (allowed 1, 2, 3, 5, 6): 1 Collision.** Capacity claims collide with the rack: a 6 lb rack says 400 lb, an 8.5 lb rack says 2,500 lb, and VEVOR's one listing says 881.8 lb and 660 lb. - **Villain (allowed A, B, D): B Hype** — Amagabeli 4 ft (most-reviewed, Family Handyman's "best budget"). Catch: Family Handyman's con (cover may not withstand sun and extreme weather), and the tested rack is a different, bigger model than this 4 ft listing. No measured failure: say so. Not a pick.
- **C1:** plant at #6 facka (a 6 lb rack, 400 lb claim); pay at #5/#1 with load-number comparison. **C2:** plant Woodhaven's top-only cover at #1 vs full wrap at #4.
