##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes five minutes.

Fill a five gallon bucket and pour it slowly into your sump pit. Watch three things. The float should rise and start the pump. The pump should empty the pit and shut itself off. And after it stops, listen. If you hear water rushing back down into the pit, your check valve is failing, and the pump is moving the same water twice. Every extra run is wear you did not need. Do this test at least twice a year. Plug the pump into its own outlet, never an extension cord. If your pump has a light on its plug, glance at it when you pass.

That test costs nothing, and it tells you the pump works before the storm does.
