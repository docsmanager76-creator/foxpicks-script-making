Here is the free upgrade, and it works with every fan on this list. Read the rating before you hang it.

Bob Vila's ceiling fan buying guide puts it plainly. Damp-rated fans handle high humidity but should not be splashed directly. Wet-rated fans have sealed motor housings that withstand rain. Dry-rated fans belong indoors only.

So check the box, and match the rating to your garage, not your living room. Then measure your ceiling. Retail buying guides say a ceiling higher than ten feet needs a longer downrod than most fans include.

Then hire an electrician, as the testers recommend, and flip the fan to reverse when winter comes so it pushes warm air back down.
