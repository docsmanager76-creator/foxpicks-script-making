##FREEUPGRADE
Now the free habit, and it works on every machine in this video except number one, which makes its own air.

Hypertherm says it plainly. The cut quality, performance and consumable life of any air plasma system are directly impacted by the quality of the air going into it.

Water is the enemy. A compressor tank collects moisture every time it runs. That moisture goes straight to your torch.

So drain the tank. At least once a day. Better still, fit an automatic drain. Then add a second filter, at least twenty feet downstream of the compressor. By then the air has cooled and dropped its water.

That is the air sensor from number six, paying off. Green light, dry air, tips that last.
