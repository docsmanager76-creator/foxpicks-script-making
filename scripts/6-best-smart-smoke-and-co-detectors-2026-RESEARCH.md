## 3. Buyer checklist
1. Smoke only, CO only, or combo — and where the gap is.
2. Sensors: photoelectric smoke, electrochemical CO.
3. UL 217 (smoke) and UL 2034 (CO) listing.
4. Power: AA, 10-year sealed lithium, hardwired + backup, plug-in + backup. CO sensor life 5-7 years vs 10-year battery.
5. How it gets online: 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi direct, gateway, or Matter-over-Thread with a border router.
6. App alerts vs subscription (optional monitoring).
7. Smart-home ecosystem: Ring, Alexa, Google Home, Matter.
8. Interconnect and silence/test button.
9. Warranty.

## 5. Hook — type 6 Category Lie
"Smart smoke and CO detector" promises one box that does both and talks to your phone. In this lineup only two units do both jobs. Others are smoke-only, CO-only, or need a hub. And some "smart" boxes on shelves just link to each other with no app at all. Open loops: villain, free upgrade, which one covers your gap.

"Smart means it watches for smoke and CO and texts me." Four of six here cover only one gas; one needs a border router; one needs a gateway.
