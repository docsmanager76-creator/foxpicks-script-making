##FREE
One habit costs nothing, and it matters no matter which of these five you buy.

Never run a standard ferrous cutoff wheel, the kind rated for steel, on stainless steel or aluminum. The wheel sheds ferrous grit as it cuts, and that grit embeds in the softer surface. On stainless, it causes rust bloom weeks later. On aluminum, it causes galvanic contamination right at the cut. Abrasive-wheel manufacturers publish this warning directly, and it's one of the most common mistakes reported in metalworking shops.

The fix costs nothing beyond picking the right wheel. Match the wheel or blade to the metal you're actually cutting, not just to the saw you own. Keep a stainless-only or aluminum-only wheel separate from the one you use on mild steel.
