## 2. Buyer-decision checklist
1. Ring system: copper crimp ring (ASTM F1807) or stainless cinch clamp (ASTM F2098). A tool for one cannot make the other joint.
2. PEX type: crimp and cinch are PEX-B-style joints; PEX-A uses expansion tools (a third class). Bob Vila: A, B, C are processes, not grades.
3. Sizes: cinch tools cover 3/8 to 1 inch in one tool; copper kits are usually 1/2 and 3/4 or swap jaws.
4. Gauge and calibration: go/no-go gauge (Bob Vila: should slip about halfway on), adjustment wrench, or factory set.
5. Hand strength and handle length (Bob Vila: 11 in or more).
6. Tight spaces: angle head, one-hand ratchet.
7. In the box: cutter, removal tool, rings or clamps, case.
8. Removal: crimp rings destroyed on removal; needs a separate blade-type tool (Bob Vila).
9. Power source and what is sold separately.
10. Warranty term.

## 9. Free upgrade (sourced)
Check every joint with the go/no-go gauge from several angles; it should slip about halfway on and stop; fully over or not at all may leak (Bob Vila). On cinch clamps confirm the ear is fully closed (PlumbingPicks, SharkBite). Match the ring to the tool before opening the box. Hold off closing the wall until each joint is checked.
