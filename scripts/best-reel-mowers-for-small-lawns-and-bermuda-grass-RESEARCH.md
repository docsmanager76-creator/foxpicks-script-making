## 5. The myth (L2)
"More blades = better" is only half true: Bob Vila — 7-blade Earthwise had the cleanest cut but was harder to push than the 5-blade models; the 5-blade Great States moved through dense grass more easily than the 18" 5-blade. **No measured numbers exist** (Bob Vila used ratings/notes only). So: myth is qualitative, no spec-leader-loses-on-measured-test pair → mark UNVERIFIED as a numeric myth; script can state it attributed to Bob Vila without numbers, or use a different myth (box-printed "up to 4 inch grass" vs UF/IFAS).
