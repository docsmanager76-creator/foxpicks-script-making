##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it matters most in hardwood.

Pre-drill and test on a scrap from the same board before you touch the cabinet.

Dense wood is where screws strip and snap. Use the screw size your slide maker gives you. A common guide for number eight screws is a pilot of about seven sixty-fourths to an eighth of an inch, with a countersink. I could not open the original pages, so trust your slide maker over me.

Rub wax or soap on the threads. Drill the slow, thin Blum holes with the stop collar set, and back the bit out often.

One more warning. Screwing a solid panel in tight holes can restrict wood movement, so use the slots your slide gives you.

That costs nothing, and it takes a minute.
