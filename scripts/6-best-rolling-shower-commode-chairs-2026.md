##FREE
One habit fixes the single most common way any chair on this list fails, and it costs nothing.

A state's own clinical criteria for shower and bath seating puts it plainly. Mobile devices must be used with caution, because the brakes can be ineffective, and the device may move during a transfer. An official clinical policy line, not a marketing claim.

Before the first real use, roll the chair onto the actual wet shower floor, not the carpet, not the dry hallway tile. Lock every wheel. Lean your full weight into it the way you will during a real transfer.

A caster that holds firm on dry ground can slide on wet tile, and that gap is invisible until someone's standing on it. That habit costs nothing, and it decides whether the first real transfer is routine or a fall.
