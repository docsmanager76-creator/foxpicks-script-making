##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it cuts snipe before you buy anything.

First, support long boards at both ends with a roller stand or an extension, and feed each board in one steady motion.

Second, keep shavings off the table. Woodgears found they make a board spring as it meets the rollers.

Third, bolt down the light machines. Bob Vila says the Craftsman needs it.

Fourth, take the last passes light, because the same write-up found small snipe on the last two inches.

Fifth, give a fifteen amp planer its own circuit, and run a shop vacuum on the dust port so chips never pile on the table or the board. Set it up once, and every pass after is easier.

That costs nothing, and it takes ten minutes. Do it before you order.
