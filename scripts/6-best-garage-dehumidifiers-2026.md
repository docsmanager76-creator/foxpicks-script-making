##FREEUP
Now the part nobody mentions, and it is the smallest thing you will buy.

Every one of these stops when its bucket fills. In a garage, nobody is there to empty it. So it fills on day two. Then it sits. You come back in spring, find your tools spotted, and blame the machine.

Set it to continuous gravity drain the day it arrives. A hose to a floor drain, a sump, or a bucket lower than the unit. It never stops again.

Check what is in the box, because it is not consistent. Two of these ship with a hose. One adds a garden hose adapter. The Frigidaire does not.

A few feet of hose is the difference between a machine that guards your tools all winter and one that worked for a day and a half.
