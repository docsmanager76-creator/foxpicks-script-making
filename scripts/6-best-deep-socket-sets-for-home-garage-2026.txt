##HOOK
Everybody says deep sockets are the tough ones. The long ones you grab when a bolt will not budge. Here is the contradiction. A deep socket is not stronger. It only reaches farther. One forum poster put it plainly. Deep sockets are for clearance, not strength. So the short socket is still the one for brute force. The deep one is for the nut at the end of a long stud. ##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it takes one minute.

First, match the finish to the tool. Polished chrome goes on a hand ratchet. Dark impact sockets go on the gun. Forum posters agree on that one.

Second, test fit before you pull. Push the socket on, and rock it. A six point should sit on the flats with almost no play.

Third, try the shallow socket first, whenever it fits. Remember the hook? The deep one is for reach, not for brute force.

Fourth, keep the deep socket square to the bolt. A tilted long socket slips more easily.

Fifth, sort your SAE and metric sockets apart, so you never put a close size on a bolt and round it.

That costs nothing, and it takes one minute.
