## 5. Hook — type 6, Category Lie
- Every combo here sells "wrist support". RTINGS, which has tested 302 keyboards, says a wrist rest should support the heel of your palm, not the wrist, because resting on the wrist puts pressure on tendons and nerves.
- No pick brands in the hook. Open loops: (1) the combo that gets the angles right, (2) the myth (raise the back feet), (3) the famous combo that was discontinued (villain), (4) the free setup habit.
- Backup: **4 Contradiction** (Reviewed's most ergonomic combo has adjustable legs that tilt up; RTINGS says negative tilt helps).

## 6. Myth — "kick out the back feet for comfort"
- RTINGS: negative tilt (front raised) reduces wrist extension; most keyboards' positive tilt adds extension. The MK850's rear feet tilt 4 or 8 degrees up (Reviewed); the Kensington KM675 EQ has front legs; the K860 (not a combo) has negative tilt.
- Proof is guidance, not a measured study; say "RTINGS says".
