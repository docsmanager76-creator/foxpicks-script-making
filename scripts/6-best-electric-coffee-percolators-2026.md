##FREE
Here's the free upgrade, and it works on every pot here. Grind your coffee coarser. Home Grounds says slightly coarser than you would use for drip. VEVOR's own listing agrees: medium-coarse, because fine grounds can clog the basket. Presto's cordless manual says the wrong grind makes bitter coffee.

Then match your grounds to your water by ounces, not cups, and watch the knob while it brews. Reviewed's explainer says to watch the transparent lid as the color builds, and that tells you more than the clock ever will.

That habit costs nothing. It decides whether a percolator tastes rich or bitter, and it is the one thing no listing can do for you.
