## STEP 0 — BUYER DECISION CHECKLIST (Cadet in-wall, fan-forced)
1. Circuit: 120 V vs 240/208 V (sets which house you can put it in), amps drawn
2. Watts at your actual voltage (240 V rating drops ~25% on 208 V)
3. Heated-area claim (and whether the maker's own numbers agree)
4. Thermostat: analog knob range vs digital, setback modes, tamper lock
5. Mounting: horizontal / vertical / ceiling, rough-in size vs 16 in studs
6. Retrofit: fits an existing Com-Pak wall can or needs a new hole
7. Fan & noise: centrifugal vs variable-speed (no dB published by Cadet for any model)
8. Safety: high-temp shutoff, auto vs manual reset, timer override
9. Build: wall can gauge, powder coat, assembled in USA
10. Ownership: warranty length, what's in the box (can + assembly + grille + thermostat)

## MYTH — "the square-foot number on the box"
Payoff: Cadet's own numbers are watts and BTU, and those agree everywhere; the room size doesn't. Buy watts for your room (rule of thumb ~10 W per sq ft — **UNVERIFIED for Cadet**, source needed or say "rule of thumb" only). Planted at #6 (200 sq ft), paid at #3 (750/600/400).
Second myth for #1: "uses 30% less energy" (maker claim) — DOE Energy Saver: every resistance heater turns ~100% of electricity into heat; any saving comes from tighter thermostat control. Say the claim attributed, then the reason.
