##FREEUPGRADE

One buying guide's own advice: match the weight class to the actual job, not the biggest number on the page.

Their contractor's rule of thumb was specific. A sixty pound air hammer for genuinely dense material. A thirty five pound range electric breaker for most homeowner demolition — old concrete steps, a patio, a section of driveway.
