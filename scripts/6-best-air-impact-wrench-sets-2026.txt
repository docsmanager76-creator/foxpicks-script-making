##FREE
Here is the free upgrade, and it works with every wrench on this list.

First, put a few drops of air-tool oil in the inlet before each use. The Drive recommends it, and it keeps the motor alive.

Second, drain the water from your compressor tank after each session. Water in the line rusts air tools from the inside.

Third, set ninety psi at the tool, not at the tank. Long hoses and cheap fittings lose pressure on the way.

Fourth, use impact sockets only. Regular chrome sockets can shatter.

Fifth, run lugs on by hand, snug them on the lowest setting, then finish with a torque wrench in a star pattern.

That is how a box number becomes real torque, and a wheel that stays on.
