## 9. Free upgrade: the wall flip check
- Fluke and Agatec manuals: set the laser about 20 ft from a wall, mark the line, rotate 180 degrees, mark again, halve the gap; more than 1/8 in at 30 ft (3 mm at 10 m) means out of calibration. DeWalt manuals: check at a distance no shorter than your working distance; field checks do not replace a service center. Honest scope: my sources are Fluke, Agatec and DeWalt-family manuals, so the script says "manufacturer manuals".
